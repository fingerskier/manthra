# The procrastinator and the hustler
are the same: neither get much done until the last minute.

The procrastinator _does_ achieve...just in the end.
The hustler achieves along the way...just at a lower level.

Both are evidence of a lack of strength and a lack of vision.

The cure is:
* Name the feeling
  * What do you feel when you think about the task at hand?
* Ask questions that start with why
  * Why are you doing this?
* Start small
  * Shrink the first step until it is easily doable
